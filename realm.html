About the Club

Real Madrid Club de Fútbol, commonly known as Real Madrid, is a professional football club based in Madrid, Spain. Founded on March 6, 1902, the club has a rich history and is widely regarded as one of the most successful and prestigious teams in the world. They play their home matches at the Santiago Bernabéu Stadium and are known for their iconic all-white home kit. With numerous domestic and international titles, including a record number of UEFA Champions League trophies, the club has a dedicated global fanbase.
